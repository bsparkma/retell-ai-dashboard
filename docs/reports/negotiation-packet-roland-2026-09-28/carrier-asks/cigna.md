## On the call

- Ask for the **fee schedule review process and the form**, not a verbal rate.
- Ask **when the schedule was last updated** and **when it next opens for review**.
- Get a **name, direct number and reference number** before hanging up.
- Anything agreed verbally means nothing until it arrives **in writing with an effective date**.
- This one is network-set: ask **which network** loads the rate and **who owns the contract**.
